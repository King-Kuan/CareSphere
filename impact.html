Global Alignment

Contributing to the Sustainable Development Goals

CareSphere's work directly contributes to the UN SDGs for a better, healthier world.

3

SDG 3 — Good Health & Wellbeing

Ensuring healthy lives and promoting wellbeing for all ages through affordable, accessible healthcare services.

4

SDG 4 — Quality Education

Providing health education and literacy programs that empower communities to make informed health decisions.

5

SDG 5 — Gender Equality

Advancing women's health rights including reproductive health, maternal care, and dignity through stigma-free services.

10

SDG 10 — Reduced Inequalities

Bridging the healthcare access gap between urban and rural, wealthy and underserved populations.

17

SDG 17 — Partnerships for Goals

Building multi-sector partnerships with healthcare providers, NGOs, and government bodies to amplify impact.

6

SDG 6 — Clean Water & Sanitation

Integrating hygiene and WASH education into community health programs to prevent waterborne diseases.
