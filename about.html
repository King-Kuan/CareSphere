About CareSphere

Our story, mission, values, and the people behind the initiative.

Our Story

Born from a Need for Inclusive Healthcare

CareSphere was founded with a clear understanding of the healthcare gaps that exist in underserved communities — especially for youth and women. Many individuals face significant barriers including limited facility access, financial constraints, unreliable health information, and deep-rooted social stigma around essential health topics.

By combining digital health technology with community-driven programs, CareSphere was built to be both a platform and a movement — one that bridges the gap between digital innovation and real-world healthcare delivery.

Today, CareSphere serves communities in Rwanda with a long-term vision of scaling across Sub-Saharan Africa, building more inclusive, resilient, and accessible health systems.

🌿

What Makes Us Different

Integrated Approach: We combine digital tools with face-to-face community engagement.

Privacy-First: All services are delivered with full confidentiality and respect.

Community-Centered: Programs are designed with, not just for, the communities we serve.

Affordable Access: Healthcare should never be out of reach due to financial barriers.

Meet Our Founder

Founder & CEO

Gedeon Niyikomeye

Gedeon Niyikomeye is a Rwandan health and wellness professional with a background in Health Science and Physiotherapy. He is a YALI Regional Leadership Center East Africa alumnus, Aspire Leaders Program alumnus, and a Tony Elumelu Foundation (TEF) Entrepreneurship Programme participant, where he was awarded seed funding capital.

Gedeon participated in the UNLEASH Innovation Lab Rwanda and completed the Global Youth Climate Training with the Global Youth Coalition, UPG Sustainability Leadership Program, FXB Climate Summer Training, and the Franklin Covey "7 Habits of Highly Effective People" program. He holds a Safeguarding Essentials Certificate from Kaya and the Humanitarian Leadership Academy, and serves as a Global Youth Ambassador at Theirworld, Global Peace Ambassador at Global Peace Chain, and volunteer coach at iDebate Rwanda.
