Frequently Asked

Common Questions

How do I access CareSphere's telemedicine service?

You can book a telemedicine consultation by filling out our contact form, calling us, or sending a WhatsApp message. We'll connect you with a qualified healthcare professional.

Are CareSphere's services confidential?

Absolutely. All health consultations, including sensitive topics like SRH and mental health, are handled with strict confidentiality and respect for your privacy.

How can I bring CareSphere to my community or school?

We welcome inquiries from schools, community organizations, and local leaders. Use the contact form above or reach out directly via WhatsApp or phone to discuss how we can partner.

How can I support CareSphere's mission?

You can support CareSphere through financial donations, volunteering, sharing our resources, or becoming a partner organization. Get in touch to learn more.
